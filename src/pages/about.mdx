	In my career, I have contributed to the development of multiple components of a distributed ledger technology.
	At [Noble](https://github.com/noble-assets/noble) I helped shape the infrastructure for
	cross-border payments. I've worked on a stablecoin automated market maker (AMM) based on the
	StableSwap invariant, and designed an abstraction layer for automated cross-chain routing on top
	of the Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC) protocol,
	the Circle Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol (CCTP), and Hyperlane.
	At Altiplanic I had the opportunity to lead a team of 5 engineers and contribute to the development
  of the [Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM)](https://github.com/evmos/evmos) in systems based on the Cosmos SDK,
  from API design and opcodes to distributed applications. Products I worked on facilitated over \$100M 
  in stablecoin transfers and \$1B in exchanged volumes.

	Before developing my passion for computers and finance, I did research at the intersection
	of machine learning and aerospace structural analysis. I was fascinated by instability
	phenomena in cylindrical shapes, and how to efficiently
	optimize them without solving complex non-linear equations. I spent around a year of my
	PhD research implementing [feed-forward neural nets](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compstruct.2019.111513)
	combined with bio-inspired optimization algorithms in the context of Computer-Aided Engineering (CAE).

	Work stuff aside, I love martial arts.
	I practiced Judo for around 13 years, and I'm now a second-dan black belt. When I was 18,
	I placed [3rd at the Italian championships](https://judoinside.com/judoka/96874/Stefano_Pitton/judo-career).
  I do believe that martial arts are a fascinating context where people can do research on their own
  body and mind.
	With the goal of learning different points of view on movement and body structure, I recently started
  studying Kung Fu, specifically the Wing Chun lineage from Leung Ting.
